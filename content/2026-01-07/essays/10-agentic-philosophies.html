Agentic Architecture Patterns

Four philosophies shaping multi-agent systems for regenerative infrastructure

1 Context Engineering

Curating what an agent can see so attention remains focused. The two strategies: Reduce (minimize noise) and Delegate (specialize context windows).

2 Orchestrator Pattern

A coordinating agent creates conditions where specialized agents can each do what they do best, like mycelium coordinating forest resources.

3 Human-in-the-Loop

Agents know their limits and pause for human judgment at critical decision points. Partnership, not supervision.

4 Prompts as Primitives

Reusable, composable prompts encode expertise. The prompt is the fundamental unit of engineering in agentic systems.

🌿

Emergence > Control

Create conditions for unexpected possibilities rather than enforcing predetermined outcomes. Let intelligence emerge from interaction.

🔗

Autonomy + Coordination

Each node maintains its own truth while contributing to collective intelligence. Decentralization enables emergence.

🤝

Partnership over Supervision

AI augments human capacity; humans contribute judgment no model can replicate. Together, something emerges neither could achieve alone.

🧠

Infrastructure is Ontological

The systems we build shape what becomes collectively thinkable. Prompts encode expertise; architecture enables or constrains possibility.
