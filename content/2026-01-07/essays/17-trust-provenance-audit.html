🔗 Resource Descriptor

Every document receives a Resource Descriptor containing its IRI, source information, visibility settings, and sha256 checksum. This metadata governs access and provides the foundation for RDF expansion and ledger anchoring.

🔍 Evidence Extraction

Citations include document_id, page, section, and confidence scores. Auditors can navigate directly to any claim and verify it independently. No summaries or paraphrases; direct quotations with coordinates.

📝 Audit Log

Every action is recorded: timestamp, actor, action_type, and details. Human decisions are attributed and traceable. The complete review process can be reconstructed from the log.

⛓ Ledger Anchoring

Content hashes are anchored via MsgAnchor in Regen Ledger's x/data module. This creates cryptographic proof of document existence at a specific point in time. Immutable, verifiable, trustless.
